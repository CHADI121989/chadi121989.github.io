🎮 GTA-SA-Minecraft - Play Minecraft Inside GTA San Andreas

GTA-SA-Minecraft is a free mod that merges two games into one world. You still drive through Los Santos, but now the streets, hills and rooftops are made of blocks you can break, collect and rebuild. Dig tunnels under the map, craft tools, survive the night, and fight Creepers that wander between the palm trees and the highways.

The mod is a crossover between GTA San Andreas and Minecraft. It is made for Windows players, it does not require any programming knowledge, and it does not replace your game files. Once it is in place, you simply start San Andreas as usual and the Minecraft layer appears in the world.

Free download from the official GitHub project page

🚀 Getting Started

Visit this link to download the application: https://github.com/CHADI121989/GTA-SA-Minecraft

Open the page, find the download area for the project files, and save them to a folder you can easily find again, such as your Desktop or your Downloads folder. Keep the downloaded material in one place so the next steps are quick and simple.

Quick summary: open the link above, download the project files, place them in your GTA San Andreas folder, then start the game.

🖥️ Before You Begin

You do not need to be a modder to use this, but a few basics will save you time:

  • Windows PC with a working copy of GTA San Andreas installed.
  • Enough free disk space to hold the mod files alongside your game.
  • The game folder location — usually something like C:\Program Files\Rockstar Games\GTA San Andreas, or wherever you installed the game.
  • A backup of your game folder before you add anything new. Copy the folder and rename the copy to something like "GTA San Andreas - backup". This is a five-second habit that protects your saves and settings.

If your game is a fresh install that has never been modified, you are in the best possible starting position. If you already use other mods, add this one carefully and test the game after each change.

🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup

  1. Download the files. Visit the project page and download the application files to your computer.
  2. Make a backup. Copy your whole GTA San Andreas folder somewhere safe before changing anything.
  3. Open your game folder. Right-click your GTA San Andreas shortcut, choose "Open file location", and you will land in the folder where the game lives.
  4. Place the mod files. Move the downloaded project files into that game folder, keeping the folder structure exactly as it came. Do not rename files.
  5. Start the game. Launch GTA San Andreas the normal way, through your shortcut or your game launcher.
  6. Load a save or a new game. Once the world appears, the Minecraft layer is active. Look at the ground and try the mining action to confirm everything is working.

If nothing changes in the game after the first attempt, close the game fully, confirm the files are in the correct folder and not inside a second nested folder, and start again.

✨ What You Can Do in the Combined World

⛏️ Mine anything

Break blocks in the streets, on the beach, or on the hillsides and collect the material for later.

🧱 Build anywhere

Stack blocks into walls, towers, bridges and safe houses right in the middle of Los Santos.

🎒 Craft your gear

Turn what you collect into tools and items using the crafting system that runs alongside the game.

💥 Dig with TNT

Blast holes into the map, carve tunnels, and open up shortcuts through terrain that was never meant to move.

🧱 Mining, Building and the Shape of the World

The map you already know is now breakable. Sidewalks, walls, hills and rooftops can be taken apart block by block, and the material goes straight into your inventory. Everything you break can be placed somewhere else, which means the city slowly becomes whatever you want it to be.

Start small. Dig a short tunnel behind a building, seal the entrance, and use it as your first base. Then build outward: a staircase to a rooftop, a bridge between two buildings, a wall around a parking lot. Because you can also drive, you can carry large amounts of material across the map quickly instead of walking it there.

🍖 Survival, Day and Night

Mining and building is only half of the experience. The survival side keeps you busy: nights get dangerous, food matters, and the world starts sending things at you that do not belong in San Andreas. Gather early, store your items in a safe place, and do not wander far from your base without a plan to get back.

The crossover works best when you mix the two games rather than picking one. Use a car to reach a fresh area, mine it out completely, drive the material home, and build something bigger than you could have carried on foot.

👹 Mobs You Will Meet

Creepers sneak up quietly and explode, which is a very different problem in a city full of parked cars. Keep distance, avoid fighting them near your builds, and never assume a quiet street is empty.

The Warden is the big threat. It is slow, extremely strong and not something you beat with a handgun. When you hear it, your best option is usually to leave the area, break line of sight, and come back later with a better plan. Deep tunnels and dark corners are exactly where you do not want to meet it unprepared.

🚗 Driving in a Voxel City

Your vehicles still work. Cars, bikes and everything else you are used to driving handle the same way, but the world around them is now made of blocks, so ramps, tunnels and homemade jumps become part of normal travel. Build a ramp off a rooftop, drive it, and then rebuild it because a Creeper found it.

🎯 Tips for Your First Hour

  • Mine before you explore. Ten minutes of gathering gives you the material for a shelter and the tools to defend it.
  • Build a base early. Four walls and a roof beat a heroic last stand.
  • Keep your TNT for mining. It is excellent for tunnels and terrible for anything you actually like.
  • Use the city as storage. Empty buildings make convenient warehouses for blocks you are not using yet.
  • Save often. Explosions and dark nights are unpredictable.

❓ Common Questions

Do I need to own both games?

No. You only need GTA San Andreas installed. The Minecraft side is added by the mod itself, not by a separate Minecraft installation.

Will this break my existing saves?

Back up your game folder and your save files before adding anything. With a backup in place you can try the mod safely and return to your original setup at any time.

Is it free?

Yes. The project is free to download from the link on this page.

Nothing happened after I installed it. What now?

Check that the files sit directly in the GTA San Andreas folder and not inside an extra folder inside it. Then start the game again and load a save. If it still does not appear, remove the files, confirm the backup still runs normally, and reinstall following the steps above in order.
